Help Center

How do I connect my wallet?

Use Connect Wallet and approve the connection in your wallet app. Never enter a recovery phrase on Momentum.

Why can't I trade with real SOL?

Trading transactions currently use Solana Devnet test funds. Real-money trading is not available.

Where can I find my transaction?

Open your wallet's activity and verify the transaction signature on the explorer for the network you used.

What if a token looks suspicious?

Verify the mint address, inspect token authority and liquidity information, and avoid signing transactions you don't understand.

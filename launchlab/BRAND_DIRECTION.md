# Momentum visual direction

Brand principle: cinematic gravitational acceleration and meteor showers, grounded in recognizable astronomical motion. Avoid generic letter M symbols, stock orbit icons, and decorative data that resembles live trading.

Homepage: an original layered meteor field with parallax, a dominant accelerating meteor, atmosphere, particles and subtle debris. Keep key text legible, mobile performance fast, and honor reduced-motion settings.

Identity: custom recognizable silhouette derived from a meteor crossing a gravity-curved trajectory; must remain clear at 24px and monochrome. The full cinematic scene is campaign art, not the favicon.

Network stage: active Solana Devnet visually distinct from roadmap-only networks. Do not imply live integrations.

Acceptance: review on 390px mobile and desktop, test reduced motion and layout overflow, validate build, verify deployed commit.
